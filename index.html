Technical documentation for operations

2024

Turned undocumented operational practice into written procedure. Most of the work was interviewing people who had never had to explain their own process out loud, then resolving the points where two of them described it differently.

Three standard operating procedures, now the reference for onboarding and handover.

Standardized partner reporting

2023

Agreed a single set of metric definitions with external partners, then rebuilt their reporting on top of it. The hard part was not the build; it was settling which definition was correct when a partner's own tool and ours had been counting the same event differently for years.

Replaced three vendor tools with one system.

Survey measurement program

2022

Brought brand-lift and media-consumption studies in house. Fixed the study design, the naming conventions, and the fielding procedure so a study became something the team runs to a written standard rather than something commissioned from scratch each time.

Reduced per-study cost by roughly 60%.

Performance textile development

2020–2021

Two years as an R&D specialist at a performance textile mill, developing prototype fabrics for tier-one outdoor and technical apparel brands. Sourcing recycled and closed-loop raw materials, qualifying each new construction against the brand's performance spec, and deciding which end uses and accounts a fabric line was built for. Audited supplier factories against REACH and bluesign requirements, and wrote the chemical compliance and facility assessments that bulk production planning ran on.

Prototype constructions carried through to bulk production across multiple seasons.

Principles

I write this list by hand every morning. The repetition puts it in memory, which is the only place a principle is any use.

Inspired by the U.S. Marine Corps leadership principles.

  1. Know yourself and seek self-improvement.
  2. Be technically and tactically proficient.
  3. Know your team and look out for their welfare.
  4. Keep your team informed.
  5. Set the example.
  6. Ensure the task is understood, supervised, and accomplished.
  7. Train your team as a unit.
  8. Make sound and timely decisions.
  9. Develop a sense of responsibility among your subordinates.
  10. Employ your command in accordance with its capabilities.
  11. Seek responsibility and take responsibility.

Principles I picked up along the way.

  1. Adjust quickly when situations change.
  2. Measure what matters, ignore the noise.
  3. A number nobody can trace is a rumour.
  4. Competence grows from consistent practice.
